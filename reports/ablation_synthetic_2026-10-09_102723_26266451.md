# Feature ablations

SYNTHETIC SOFTWARE VALIDATION ONLY — invented plants, labels and pixels. Not project performance.

Each variant tunes models only inside inner grouped folds and uses identical outer partitions. Comparing variants on outer results is exploratory, not an unbiased estimate after selecting a feature set. No feature or production-model selection is performed.

| Variant | Outer Macro F1 | Balanced accuracy |

Feature definitions: additions are independent comparisons against the baseline, not cumulative. Chromaticity is mean pixelwise R/(R+G+B), G/(R+G+B), with black pixels assigned zero. LAB uses floating-point OpenCV CIELAB a*/b* means and population standard deviations. Oriented ratio is minAreaRect short/long side, replacing the axis-aligned ratio. No claim of illumination invariance or direct curl measurement.

Full metrics and fold memberships are in the companion JSON. Production features and model remain unchanged.
